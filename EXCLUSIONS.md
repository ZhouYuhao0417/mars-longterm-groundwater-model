# Excluded artifacts and claims

The medium scenario is not a completed 20-year two-dimensional run. Its surviving status record is retained at `data/excluded/medium_summary_incomplete.json` for auditability, with `complete=false`, `paper_usable=false` and `completed_years=3.72798923954534`.

The following are intentionally not distributed as publication results:

- medium-scenario depth, arrival and duration rasters;
- any map derived from the incomplete medium checkpoint;
- outputs from the withdrawn semi-distributed storage-routing/HAND model;
- inferred outlines or labels for the two troughs;
- screen-pixel measurements from the interactive preview.

The medium parameter set remains visible as a prescribed hydrograph for sensitivity design only. It must not be described as a completed spatial result.
